Community Guidelines

The omachrome gallery is a place to share colour recipes for the OM System OM-3. These guidelines keep it useful, friendly and safe for everyone.

Last updated: November 2026

01

Recipes, nothing else

A recipe is a set of camera settings with a name and a short description. The gallery has no photos, links or comments.

02

Share what's yours

Publish recipes you made, or have permission to share. Be honest about what a recipe is and who made it.

03

Speak up

Anyone can report a recipe from the app. We review reports and remove what breaks these guidelines.

Who can publish

Browsing the gallery is free for everyone. Publishing is for omachrome Lab subscribers. You publish under a display name you choose (2 to 30 characters). We don't ask for your real name, and we never receive your email address.

What you can publish

  • Colour recipes you created yourself, or that you have permission to share
  • A clear, honest name (up to 40 characters) and an optional description (up to 280 characters)
  • Up to three tags from the gallery's list

Names and descriptions can use letters, numbers, spaces and basic punctuation. Emoji, links and web addresses aren't allowed.

What you can't publish

  • Recipes copied from another creator without their permission, or that a creator has asked not to be shared
  • Names or descriptions that are offensive, hateful, harassing or sexually explicit, or that encourage violence or illegal activity
  • Spam, adverts or promotion of any kind
  • Anything that pretends to be another person or suggests an endorsement by OM System or omachrome
  • Personal information about anyone
  • Attempts to abuse the service, such as bulk publishing, probing for weaknesses or getting around limits

A simple word filter checks names and descriptions automatically. It isn't perfect, so we also rely on reports and our own review.

How publishing works

Your first three recipes are reviewed by us before they appear in the gallery. A recipe with exactly the same settings as another creator's published recipe is also held for review. Once three of your recipes have been approved, new ones go live straight away. We can still review, hide or remove any recipe at any time.

Daily and overall limits apply to how many recipes you can publish, to keep the gallery tidy.

Reporting a recipe

Anyone can report a recipe from its page in the app. You don't need a subscription. Choose a reason (offensive, spam, copied or other) and add a note if you like. When several different people report the same recipe, it's hidden automatically while we review it.

If you're a creator and a recipe copies your work, email us with a link to the recipe and we'll look at it promptly.

If you'd rather not see someone's recipes, you can block that publisher in the app. This only affects your own device, and they aren't told.

Removing a recipe

Omachrome Lab subscribers can delete their own recipes from My Published Recipes in the app. If your subscription has lapsed, your published recipes stay in the gallery. To have one removed, email hello@omachrome.com with its link, or the display name you published under.

Removed recipes are permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.

When the guidelines are broken

We have no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. Depending on how serious it is, we may hide or remove a recipe, limit what a publisher can do, or ban them, with or without warning. If you think we got it wrong, tell us and we'll take another look.

Contact

To report a problem, ask for a recipe to be removed, or tell us how we can do better:

hello@omachrome.com

These guidelines work alongside our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy, and may change over time. We'll update the date above when they do.
