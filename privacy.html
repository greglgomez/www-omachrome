Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Introduction

omachrome ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website (omachrome.com).

omachrome is operated by Gregorio Luiz Gomez, 101 Ladysmith Road, Exeter, EX1 2PS, United Kingdom. Contact: hello@omachrome.com

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and information collected automatically through your use of our app and website:

  • Analytics data: device information (model, OS version), IP address, unique user identifiers (such as cookie IDs), features accessed, app interactions, session recordings, and heatmap data
  • Performance data: crash reports and performance metrics
  • Newsletter sign-ups: email address, if you choose to subscribe to our newsletter
  • Recipe gallery data: if you use the gallery, the information described under "The omachrome Recipe Gallery" below

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our app and services
  • Understand and analyze usage patterns via product analytics
  • Test and deploy new features using feature flags
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical and security issues
  • Send you our newsletter and product updates (if you have subscribed)

Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party processors. Each is bound by a data processing agreement or equivalent data protection terms.

PostHog (Product Analytics)

We use PostHog Inc. (2261 Market Street #4008, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA) for product analytics, including insights, heatmaps, session recording, and feature flags. PostHog processes data on our behalf as a data processor under a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

PostHog may process the following data: name, email address, IP address, unique user IDs (such as cookie IDs), device and usage information, and any content you interact with in the app.

PostHog does not sell your personal data and does not use your data to train AI models. Data may be stored and processed in the United States and other locations listed on PostHog's subprocessor page. PostHog participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, the UK Extension to the EU-US DPF, and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. International transfers of EU, UK, and Swiss personal data are also covered by Standard Contractual Clauses.

For more information, see PostHog's Privacy Policy.

Loops (Newsletter)

If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is collected and processed by Loops (loops.so). Loops is our email platform and acts as a data processor on our behalf. Your email address is used solely to send you omachrome newsletters and product updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email.

For more information, see Loops's Privacy Policy.

Apple (App Store and Subscriptions)

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. We don't receive your name, email address, Apple ID or payment details. For omachrome Lab, your device sends us a signed confirmation from Apple, which we check is genuine (see below).

Cloudflare (Gallery Hosting)

The recipe gallery and its recipe pages run on Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers and its database service). Cloudflare processes requests and stores gallery data on our behalf as a data processor under its standard data processing terms. Data may be processed in the United States and other places where Cloudflare operates.

For more information, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

The omachrome Recipe Gallery

The gallery lets people browse, save and (with omachrome Lab) publish colour recipes. Recipes are settings, a name and a description. There are no photos.

Browsing and recipe links

You don't need an account to browse the gallery or open a recipe link. Like any website, our servers receive your IP address and standard request details when you connect. We don't store IP addresses in our database. To stop abuse we count requests using a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address that can't be turned back into the address, and we delete those counters after about two days. The recipe pages on r.omachrome.com set no cookies of their own and run no analytics or scripts.

Publishing with omachrome Lab

If you publish, we create a random publisher ID for you. It isn't linked to your name or email address. We store:

  • The display name you choose, which is public
  • The recipes you publish (name, description, tags and settings) and when, which are public
  • The recipes you've saved, so a recipe's save count stays accurate
  • Counts we need to apply daily limits and moderation

To recognise you between sessions, your device sends us a signed confirmation of your App Store subscription. We check it is genuine and store only a one-way hash derived from it. We don't keep the confirmation itself.

Reports and moderation

When you report a recipe we store the reason, any note you write, and a one-way hash that stops the same person reporting the same recipe repeatedly. We don't store your IP address or name with a report. Only we can see reports and notes. We keep a log of moderation actions (such as hiding or removing a recipe or banning a publisher) using recipe and publisher IDs.

Gallery analytics

Using the gallery in the app (for example opening it or a recipe) is measured with the same app analytics described above. It doesn't include the contents of recipes.

Data Security

We and our service providers implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process your personal data outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Where required by law (for example, for users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or participation in recognized data transfer frameworks.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability (where applicable)
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (EEA/UK users)

Because gallery publisher IDs are random, to find your gallery data we may ask for details such as your display name or links to your recipes. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@omachrome.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Newsletter subscribers' email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe. Analytics data retention is governed by our agreement with PostHog.

Gallery data: published recipes stay until you or we remove them. Removed recipes are permanently deleted within 30 days, together with their saves and reports. Request counters are deleted after about two days. The moderation log, which contains only IDs and the action taken, is kept.
